# LookFinesse — Brand Name Archive

> Historical brand name exploration. **LookFinesse** is the active product name.
> This doc preserves earlier naming research for reference.

---

## Category 1 — Premium & Aspirational

Names that signal quality, exclusivity, and aspiration for a creator-first African marketplace.

| Name | Rationale |
|------|-----------|
| **VYBN** | Extension of VYB — adds depth, feels like a next-gen platform name. Short, typeable, global. |
| **Sora** | Japanese for "sky" — conveys limitlessness and aspiration. 4 letters, memorable, URL-safe. |
| **Lux Drop** | Directly signals premium product drops. Appealing to fashion-forward Africans. |
| **Kovu** | Swahili-adjacent, means "scar" (resilience) in Lion King. Unique, African, strong. |
| **Opulé** | French "opulence" — signals luxury, works as a beauty/fashion brand. Stylish accent. |
| **Zuri Commerce** | "Zuri" = beautiful in Swahili. Positioned for the African aesthetic, female-skewing. |
| **LNKD Kreators** | Plays on "linked" — emphasises the creator ecosystem and commerce connection. |
| **Vanta** | Ultra-black finish — implies depth, innovation, modernity. Great for dark-UI brand. |

## Category 2 — African Identity & Gen-Z Energy

Names rooted in East/West African culture that Gen-Z users will identify with.

| Name | Rationale |
|------|-----------|

| **Moto** | Swahili for "fire" — energy, heat, vitality. Short. 4 letters. Easy to chant. |
| **Jua** | Swahili for "sun" — warmth, positivity. Matches the Nairobi vibe perfectly. |
| **Nadhifu** | Swahili for "clean/neat" — works for a premium lifestyle commerce brand. |

| **Duka Yetu** | "Our shop" in Swahili — community ownership narrative. Feels local but proud. |

| **Chapchap** | East African slang for "quickly" — speed, efficiency, Gen-Z energy. Fun to say. |

Names that travel globally without losing identity.

## Final Recommendation

If launching as a **premium creator marketplace** with African identity:
→ **SOKO** (cultural authority) or **VYBN** (brand evolution)

If launching with **Gen-Z social-first** energy:
→ **Moto** or **Jua** (4-letter African words that globalise easily)

If going **international from day one**:
→ **Kreato** or **Novu** (clean, trademarkable, URL-safe)
